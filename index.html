Handsight — Real-Time Sign Language to Speech Translator

Bridging sign language and spoken communication with on-device vision + speech synthesis

A real time sign language translation prototype that captures hand gestures via webcam, classifies them with a MobileNetV2 model through cvzone and OpenCV, and instantly converts the recognized text into natural speech using gTTS. It is designed to make everyday communication more inclusive and accessible by reducing barriers between signers and non signers.

Research, AI and Scientific Publications

My research bridges applied machine learning, statistical modeling and domain-driven experimentation to turn complex data into reliable, explainable and deployable solutions. Spanning time series forecasting, risk assessment, natural language processing, computer vision and explainable AI, the work emphasizes rigorous evaluation, methodological transparency and responsible translation from peer-reviewed IEEE and SINTA-indexed publications to practical systems that support informed decision making.

01
IEEE ICITRI 2026 · Medical Imaging · Explainable AI

Lightweight CNN Benchmark for Liver Fibrosis Ultrasound Classification with Explainable AI — ICITRI 2026

Benchmark of nine CNN architectures for five-class liver fibrosis staging (METAVIR F0–F4) using 6,323 B-mode ultrasound images from 955 patients with multi-seed evaluation and Grad-CAM explainable AI. Evaluated across 34 valid runs with Accuracy, Macro-F1, QWK, MASE, Adjacent/Severe Error Rates, ECE plus efficiency metrics (model size, FLOPs, latency, memory). Top cluster led by DenseNet-121 (98.3% Accuracy, 97.5% Macro-F1, 0.995 QWK), FastNet-T1 and RepViT-M1.0 with competitive performance at lower cost; MobileNetV4-Small best compact alternative.

IEEE ICITRI 2026 · Medical Imaging · Vision Transformers

Comparative Evaluation of Transformer and Lightweight Vision Models for Glaucoma Detection from Fundus Images — ICITRI 2026

Comparative benchmark of transformer and lightweight vision models for glaucoma detection from fundus images. Evaluated with Accuracy, Macro-F1, QWK, MASE, Adjacent and Severe Error Rates and ECE to assess diagnostic reliability and calibration. Highlights trade-offs between high-capacity transformers and efficient lightweight models for scalable ophthalmic screening. Presented at The 7th ICITRI 2026 and published in IEEE Xplore.
